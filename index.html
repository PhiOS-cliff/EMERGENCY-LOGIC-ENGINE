What ELE Does

Emergency operators condense conditions into movement.

Emergency managers solve problems differently. They identify the condition, determine ownership, establish priorities, isolate bottlenecks, and locate the next procedural minimum.

ELE applies Emergency Logic to General AI.

What ELE Is and Is Not
ELE IS

A procedural operating layer

It sits on top of General AI and transposes default reasoning into Emergency Logic.

ELE IS NOT

A replacement for authority or judgment

It does not replace field verification, professional review, or official command.

How ELE Is Used

ELE operates on top of General AI.

The host may be GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or another compatible model. General AI remains the source environment. ELE changes the logic applied inside it.
