What ELE Is

General AI expands. Emergency Logic compresses.

General AI remains the underlying reasoning environment. ELE changes the procedural logic applied to that environment when an emergency condition is active.

General AI
  • Expands possibilities
  • Explores interpretations
  • Generates explanations and options
  • Supports broad reasoning and discussion
Emergency Logic
  • Identifies the condition
  • Locates ownership
  • Establishes priority
  • Exposes bottlenecks
  • Finds procedural minimums
  • Compresses toward movement
